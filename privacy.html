Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the Pixaru Scan app for Android (“the app”, package com.pixaru.scan) handles information. The app is made by Pixaru, an independent developer based in India (“we”, “us”).

In short: your documents stay on your phone. We have no account system and no servers for your files, and we never upload, read or sell your documents. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described below. Pro users see no ads.

1. Your documents

Scans, photos, ID cards, vehicle papers, exam files, PDFs, names, expiry dates and settings you create are saved only inside the app on your phone. We cannot see or access them.

  • They leave your phone only when you choose to share or download them, for example to WhatsApp, email, Google Drive or your Download folder. The app or service you send them to handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, your documents are deleted. We cannot recover them.
  • Android keeps app data private to the app. If you use App lock (Pro), a PIN or your phone’s fingerprint is needed to open the app.

2. Permissions

PermissionWhy the app uses it
CameraTo scan documents, ID cards, photos and signatures. Images are processed on your phone.
Photos and files (you choose)To open the photos or PDFs you pick from your gallery or files. The app only reads the files you select.
Storage (Android 9 and older)To save downloaded files to your Download / Pixaru Scan folder.
NotificationsTo remind you before an ID, licence, insurance or PUC expires. Reminders are scheduled on your phone only.
Biometric (Pro, optional)To unlock the app with your fingerprint. Android checks your fingerprint; the app never receives fingerprint data.
InternetTo show ads, check Pro purchases with Google Play, download the scanner module once, and open the official websites you choose.

3. Camera scanner (Google ML Kit)

The camera scanner uses Google’s ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play services. Pages are processed on your phone. Google Play services may collect limited technical data about the scanner’s performance under Google’s privacy policy.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location based on your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app, ad interactions, and crash and performance data.

Ads may be personalised based on this information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

No ads are shown to Pro users. Ads never see your documents.

5. Purchases (Pixaru Scan Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card, UPI or bank details. The app receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, which it keeps on your phone to unlock Pro and checks with Google Play to restore Pro on a new phone.

6. Official websites

The “Check online” links on a vehicle page open official Government of India websites (such as Parivahan) in your browser. Before opening, the app may copy your vehicle number to your phone’s clipboard so you can paste it. We do not send it anywhere. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

7. What we collect

We, Pixaru, do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information or documents. We do not use our own analytics. The only data collection is done by Google for ads, purchases and Google Play services, as described above.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

8. Children

The app is meant for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with settings suitable for a general audience.

9. Your rights and deleting data

Because your documents are stored only on your phone, you control them fully. You can delete any document in the app, delete everything in Settings → Delete all documents, or uninstall the app. See how to delete your data. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may contact us with any question or grievance about your data.

10. Security

Your files are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage is 100% secure, so please keep your phone locked and use App lock for extra protection.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
