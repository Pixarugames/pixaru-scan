Delete your data

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pixaru Scan has no accounts and no servers for your documents, so all your data is on your own phone and you can delete it at any time.

Delete documents in the app

  • One document: open it and tap the delete (bin) button.
  • A vehicle: open the vehicle, tap ⋮ → Remove vehicle.
  • Everything: Settings → Delete all documents.

Delete everything at once

Uninstall the app, or go to your phone’s Settings → Apps → Pixaru Scan → Storage → Clear storage. This removes all documents, settings, reminders and your App lock PIN from the phone. Files you already downloaded to your Download / Pixaru Scan folder stay there until you delete them in your Files app.

Advertising data

Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob. To delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, open your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google’s handling of ad data is described in Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Your Pro purchase is recorded by Google Play on your Google account. You can see it in the Play Store → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history.

Emails to us

If you emailed us and want that conversation deleted, write to pixarugames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my emails”. We will delete it within 30 days.
