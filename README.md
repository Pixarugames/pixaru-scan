## Play Console – Data safety answers (with AdMob and Play Billing)

- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes** (the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data).
- Is all data encrypted in transit? **Yes**.
- Do you provide a way to request deletion? **Yes** – https://pixarugames.github.io/pixaru-scan/delete-data.html
- Data types (collected by the Ads SDK, shared with Google for advertising, fraud prevention and analytics):
  - Location → Approximate location (from IP)
  - Device or other IDs → Advertising ID
  - App activity → App interactions
  - App info and performance → Crash logs, Diagnostics
- Documents, photos and files: **not collected** (they stay on the device; sharing is started by the user).
- Purchase history: handled by Google Play, not collected by the app.

## Play Console – other declarations
- Ads: **Yes, contains ads**.
- Target audience: **13–15, 16–17, 18+** (exam students). Do not choose under 13.
- Government apps: the app is **not** a government app; the store listing must say it is not affiliated with any government body.
